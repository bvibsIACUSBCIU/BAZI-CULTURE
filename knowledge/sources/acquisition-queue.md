## Approved Search Venues

Searches may use:

- national and university library catalogs;
- scan-backed Wikisource editions;
- Internet Archive institutional holdings;
- Chinese Text Project for bibliographic leads only;
- publisher and lawful book-retailer records;
- lawfully borrowed or purchased physical/digital editions.

Anonymous drives, piracy indexes, and untraceable OCR bundles are excluded.

## Owner Input

No owner-provided content is required yet.

- a required edition is available only through lawful purchase or borrowing;
- a reviewer must choose between competing recensions;
- access to a library holding requires the owner's account or physical visit.
